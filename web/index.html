Field guide / getting started

Learn the cycle.
Run the site.

Start with free digging, then take on a timed contract. Each shift is a chance to explore how ground, equipment, and hauling work together.

01 / How to play

From the first bite to the last truck.

  1. Choose your ground

    Pick one of six regions on the world map. Read the ground conditions, choose your equipment spread, and select Mobilize to site. Choose Free dig / no clock to practice.

  2. Time your dig

    Hold Space or the gold button to dig, load, and back up to fresh material at pipe grade. Steady bites use 80% capacity. Release in the striped zone for a full single bite and a cash bonus. Release to stop repeating; the current action finishes.

  3. Load and haul

    Keep Space held to load automatically, or press Load truck after a single cut. Full trucks leave automatically and count toward your target. Material that does not fit stays in the bucket.

  4. Keep the spread moving

    Dig the next bucket while the truck returns. Watch the active soil layer: deeper material changes the timing zone and digging speed. Dispatch your target before the clock runs out to unlock the next contract.

02 / At the controls

Mouse, keyboard, or touch.

Operate
Press Space anywhere in the game to start, resume, dig, load, or move to the next cut. Hold to keep working. A tap during an animation queues one next action. Space clears a crew stop and recovers a blocked truck. Typing fields and the guide keep normal keyboard behavior. Use Enter to activate a focused toolbar control. Leaving the game or pressing P cancels queued input.
Move the machine
Hold Up to drive toward the bucket; hold Down to reverse. Release to stop. Left and Right turn the machine. Trench assist makes each turn 90 degrees so the machine stays aligned. Trench assist also limits cuts to the 0.90 m pipe bed. Turn it off for continuous steering and deeper excavation. These directions follow the machine, even when you orbit the camera. Travel is locked during digging, loading, and pipe work.
Look around
Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Use Site, Machine, or Overhead to reset the camera.
Review the plan
Site plan overlays a proposed pipe corridor and target depth on the actual ground. Blue boxes are planned cuts; gold boxes are on grade; green boxes contain pipe. Plan from bucket aligns a new plan with the machine. Plans save with the site. This is a game layout, not an imported project plan.
Clear the crew
After pipe work, call Clear crew and wait for their acknowledgment. Workers in the swing or travel zone stop equipment. Open trenches, exposed pipe and stored bundles block the haul path; use Switch truck side to approach from the other side, or reposition to a clear loading pad. Cancel truck move parks a truck if its route becomes blocked; reposition, then call it again. These are equipment-based game zones, not universal safety distances.
Take a break
Select Pause, or press P with the scene or gold button focused. The guide and world map also pause your shift. Select Resume shift when ready.
Fill the screen
Select Expand game to fill the browser window. Select Restore view or press Esc to return. Fullscreen also hides the browser controls where supported. Movement and digging work in both views.
Build your spread
Clean bites and full trucks earn game cash. Buy a larger bucket for $200 or a second haul unit for $150 while the machine is idle.

Each region and equipment spread keeps a separate saved site. Excavation, pipe, upgrades, crew experience and active work carry into the next shift. Reloaded shifts resume paused. A new shift keeps the site.

03 / Construction concepts

What the game helps you explore.

Take a line through construction

Choose Build a utility line on the world screen. Space advances a guided 12 m project through set-out, excavation, formation checks, bedding, pipe placement, inspection, four backfill lifts and compaction at each station. Wet-ground sites need pumping. Order materials ahead of the crew to avoid waiting. Choose a work method and compare elapsed time, passes and scenario costs. The final report counts accepted sections, not just tonnes moved.

These stages introduce the sequence of work. Read the OSHA excavation requirements and PPI installation guidance for real-world context; actual projects follow their engineered design and local requirements.

Production is a connected cycle

Digging, loading, and hauling all take time. Watch when the excavator waits for a truck. A faster bucket alone may not remove the delay.

Try it: In free dig, compare truck waiting time before and after buying the second haul unit.

Ground conditions change the work

The active layer shifts as you excavate. Sand, gravel, clay, and fractured rock have different resistance presets, so the same input does not always produce the same cycle.

Try it: Use the same fleet in Dubai and Iceland. Compare the timing zone and the time each cut takes.

Recover and keep working

Space always requests the next action. Crew in the equipment zone move clear before the cycle continues. A blocked or parked truck gets a short recovery onto a clear loading lane. At the pad boundary, the spread moves to fresh ground. Recovery preserves excavation, installed pipe, payload and earnings. These are arcade recovery rules, not real equipment procedures.

Build a continuous pipe run

Release Space when the crew says the bed is ready so you can install pipe before advancing. With an empty bucket, select Install 2 m pipe. Select Back up 2 m to align the next section, then dig, load, and install again. Use Connect joint when two pipe ends meet. The crew works visibly in your excavated trench.

Know what the numbers mean

All t are metric tonnes; m3 is cubic metres of undisturbed ground. Each cut removes grid volume and converts it to payload using that bite's density preset: earth 1.8, clay 1.7, sand 1.6, gravel 1.9, basalt 2.2 t/m3. Loading and hauling preserve mass. Loose-volume swell is not modeled. Dimensions show the current 2 m section; volume and removed mass cover the whole saved site.

Three distinct 2 m sections on grade unlock throttle. Eco uses less fuel and slower hydraulics; Boost cycles faster at higher L/h. Fuel and RPM are game presets. Paused shifts use no fuel.

Match the bucket to the truck

Payload is the mass carried in the bucket or truck. In the starting utility spread, a 2.5 t full bucket takes eight loads to fill a 20 t truck. Smaller bites need more cycles.

Try it: Fill a truck with clean bites, then repeat with short bites. Count the loads and compare the elapsed time.

Use the game to ask better field questions.

This is an introduction to earthwork production, not equipment training or a cost estimate. Timing, prices, soil layers, and capacities are simplified game presets. Real work also requires site investigation, utility locating, safety planning, and supervised training.

    Field log and learning notes

      Work in sequence: check the formation, place bedding, set and inspect pipe, then place and compact each lift. Pass counts and costs are scenario values; they do not represent field density tests or operating specifications. Excavated spoil and imported fill are tracked separately.

      Your spread

      Clean bites earn cash. Keep full trucks leaving the cut.

      Crew / training and breaks

      Levels run from 1 to 10. Work and drills earn XP. Operator levels shorten cuts; joiner levels speed up pipe work; spotting practice improves crew travel. Lunch restores energy. Safety zones apply at every level.

      Operator notes & controls

      Up drives toward the bucket; Down reverses; Left/Right turn. Trench assist locks turns to 90 degrees. Back up 2 m aligns the next trench section. Stop to dig at the bucket location; cuts remain in the ground as you move. The support truck follows your spread. Hold Space or the gold button to dig, load and advance. Tap during a cycle to queue one next action. Release in the marked zone for a full single bite. Trench assist limits cuts to the 0.90 m bed; continued input backs up 2 m for the next cut. Stop at grade to install pipe. Space clears crew stops and recovers blocked trucks while preserving your work. Only dispatched full trucks count. Leftovers stay in the bucket. Dig the next bite while trucks return. Deeper layers change resistance and the timing window.

      Expand game fills the browser window; Restore view or Esc exits. Drag the view to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Choose Site, Machine or Overhead for a camera preset. Press P to pause. Opening the world map pauses your active job; returning to the same site resumes it. Changing sites or fleets opens that spread’s saved job, or a fresh site on the first visit. Upgrades carry between contracts on the same site.
